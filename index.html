Requirement inheritance

REQUIREMENTS ENGINE

Industry → Organisation → Client / Contract → Operational Unit → Role → Duty → Person

Lower levels can add stronger requirements without silently weakening mandatory requirements above them. A change at one level recalculates affected people and work eligibility.
